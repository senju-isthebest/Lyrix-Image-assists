LyrixBot

LyrixBot is a Discord bot I built for community servers that need moderation, support tickets and a proper way to manage their staff in one place. Alongside that it has community tools like giveaways and suggestions, a set of Islamic features, and a web dashboard for configuring the main systems.

This page goes through each area with screenshots from the bot itself. Commands are shown in code style, and the bot has 72 slash commands in total. /help lists them inside Discord.

Moderation & Security

The moderation commands cover the usual actions, and cases tie them together so staff can look back at what happened to a member and why.

Actions

/ban accepts an optional duration (for temporary bans) and how many days of messages to delete. /kick, /timeout, /untimeout, /unban and /warn work the way you would expect. /purge bulk-deletes messages, and can be limited to one member.

Cases, warnings and notes

/case can show a single case, change its reason, list every case for a member (history) or show the latest cases in the server. /warnings lists, removes or clears a member's warnings. /note keeps private staff notes on a member, and /watchlist tracks members staff want to keep an eye on.

Ban appeals

/appeal-review lists pending ban appeals. Approving one unbans the user automatically, denying it keeps the ban, and both accept an optional note.

Verification gate

/setup-verification posts a verification button in a channel. You pick the verified role, an optional unverified role, and a minimum account age in hours for new members.

Server backups

/server-backup takes a snapshot of your roles and channels. If something gets deleted, restore brings back the missing roles and channels from a backup (you choose roles, channels or both). It can also list and delete backups.

/snipe shows the most recently deleted message in a channel.

The verification panel (Arabic interface).

Moderation can also be done from the web dashboard. The Moderation Actions page lets staff queue a warn, timeout or ban by member ID. The bot carries it out within a few seconds using the same rules as the commands, so you need a moderator role and have to outrank the target. Temporary bans are listed with their unban time.

Moderation Actions in the dashboard.

The Moderation page holds the mod-log channel, an invite tracker toggle and a cooldown for suggestions. It also controls optional text commands such as !warn with a custom prefix, where each one can be switched on or off and shares the channel, role and cooldown rules of its slash command. Prefix-free aliases let you map a word of your choice to a moderation command.

Ticket Systems

LyrixBot has two ticket systems. They are separate, and they differ in how a member starts a ticket and where the settings live, so choose the one that matches how your server handles support.

A. Department tickets

The member picks a department before anything is created, so the request reaches the right team from the start. Add departments with /setup-ticket-departments add (name, category, staff role and an optional emoji), remove them with remove, and publish the dropdown panel in a channel with post.

The departments in the screenshot (General Support, Staff/Member Reports, Gifts Claim) are examples. You define your own.

B. Dashboard-configured tickets

Members open a ticket from a button panel that /setup-tickets posts in the channel you choose, with an optional title and description. The behaviour of these tickets is configured in the dashboard under System Config, in the Ticket System section:

  • the category tickets are created in, the staff role, and a log channel
  • staff points when a ticket is claimed (0 disables it), with an option to give them only when the ticket is closed
  • maximum open tickets per member (1 to 10)
  • auto-close after a set number of hours without activity, plus a grace period (1 to 168 hours) after the inactivity warning
  • a target first-response time in minutes, with an optional alert to staff when it is exceeded
  • how many days a closed ticket can still be reopened (0 disables reopening)

Tools for ticket staff

/ticket priority sets a ticket's priority (low, normal, high or urgent) from inside the ticket channel, and /ticket add and /ticket remove manage who can see it. /ticket reopen brings a closed ticket back in a new channel. /ticketstaff adds or removes individual members as ticket staff without needing a role, and /tag stores canned responses staff can send with use.

Staff Applications

Staff applications run from a panel you post in a channel, and you control when they are open.

Commands

  • /setup-apply posts the application panel in the channel you choose.
  • /apply-toggle opens or closes applications.
  • /result accepts or rejects an applicant, with an optional reason.
  • /apply-blacklist stops a member from applying (add, remove, list).
  • /apply-publish publishes announcements for newly accepted applicants.

In the dashboard

The Apply page shows the question form. The sidebar also has Applications, Application Types and Form Builder pages, and the admin overview keeps a count of accepted applications that are still waiting to be published, and of blacklisted members.

Staff Points & Management

Staff activity is tracked with points. The leaderboard, profiles and achievements all show that same data, and the commands below let admins adjust it.

Points

/points shows your points or another staff member's. Admins can change them with /addpoints, /removepoints, /setpoints and /resetpoints.

Bonus multipliers

/points-bonus starts a temporary multiplier now (now) or at a later time (schedule). /points-bonus-status checks it and /points-bonus-stop ends it immediately.

Seasons

/season end archives the current leaderboard and resets everyone's points. Past seasons can be listed and their leaderboards viewed again.

Level roles

/levelroles gives a reward role automatically when someone reaches a points threshold. You add, remove and list the thresholds.

Promotions

/request-promotion lets a staff member ask for a promotion once they meet the requirements. /demote moves a staff member down one rank, with an optional reason.

Absences

/absence registers an absence with a number of days and a reason, ends it early, lists who is currently absent and shows the status of a specific member.

/leaderboard shows the staff ranking page by page, /profile shows a staff member's rank, total points and progress, and /achievements lists unlocked and locked achievements. On the server in these screenshots, a point comes from every 300 messages or 10 hours in voice, and the profile shows progress toward the next one.

Ranks, Daily Rewards & Shop

Progression

/rank shows a visual rank card for you or another member. /daily claims a daily reward and keeps a streak going, and /quests tracks your daily quest progress.

Redeem codes

Admins create points codes with /redeem-admin (points, maximum uses, a custom code and an expiry in days), and members claim them with /redeem. Codes can also be listed and deleted.

Server shop

/shop lets members browse what the server sells. Products are added on the dashboard Shop page with a name, price, description, payment instructions and a reward. A member buys by opening a ticket, staff confirm that payment happened outside Discord, and the bot delivers the reward automatically if it is a role. The page also has an option for accepting ProBot credits with automatic confirmation.

Community & Engagement

Giveaways

/giveaway start takes a prize, duration and number of winners, and can require a role or a minimum number of points to enter. Members join with a button. /giveaway end finishes one early and /giveaway reroll picks new winners.

Suggestions

/suggest posts a suggestion with For and Against votes, a status, buttons to consider, approve or deny it, and a discussion thread. /suggestion delete removes one (the author while it is pending, staff at any time).

Polls and confessions

/poll creates a poll from a question and a list of options, with an optional duration. /confess sends an anonymous message to the server.

Birthdays and invites

/birthday sets, removes and lists upcoming birthdays. /invites shows a member's invite count and a leaderboard of top inviters.

Roles and auto-replies

/reaction-roles links an emoji on a message to a role. /autoresponder replies automatically when a message contains, or exactly matches, a trigger you set. These can also be managed from the dashboard.

Islamic Features

The Islamic features are configured with /setup-islamic, which has three parts: the radio voice channel, an automatic azkar channel, and prayer time and adhan alerts. The interface in these screenshots is Arabic.

Quran in voice channels

/play-quran plays a surah with the reciter you choose, with options for volume, looping and adding it to the queue. /quran-queue shows, extends or clears the queue, /quran-skip moves to the next surah, and /quran-stop stops playback and leaves the voice channel. /list-reciters lists the available reciters and can filter by Hafs or Warsh.

Quran radio

/radio opens a control panel for the radio. It has dropdowns for Quran recitation, Azkar, Tafsir and Seerah, and buttons to play, refresh and check status. The voice channel it plays in is chosen with /setup-islamic radio.

Verses, azkar and prayer times

/quran shows a verse with its explanation (choose the surah and ayah). /azkar sends a remembrance or supplication, and can be limited to a category such as morning, evening, sleep, travel or after prayer. /prayer shows prayer times for a country and city.

Web Dashboard

The dashboard is a web panel for the systems described above. It does not replace the commands, and not everything is configured there, but most of the day-to-day setup is. The pages below are the ones shown in the screenshots.

Admin overview

Shows how far along the server setup is (the Setup Wizard walks through the basics), counters for staff with points, accepted applications waiting to be published, active absences and blacklisted members, and shortcuts to commands, applications, points, AutoMod, tickets, giveaways and auto-responders.

Admin overview.

Command management

Lists every command with its category and how many times it was used in the last 30 days. For each one you can enable or disable it, limit it to specific channels or roles, and set a cooldown in seconds. You can search, filter by category and change several commands at once.

Inbox

Collects things that need attention in one place. In the screenshot a new suggestion has arrived and can be marked as read.

Languages & Utilities

Languages

LyrixBot replies in English, Arabic and French. Each member can choose the language the bot uses with them through /settings language, or follow the server default. /settings dm turns the bot's direct messages (application results, promotions and similar) on or off.

Small utilities

/translate translates text into another language. /afk sets an AFK status and replies automatically when you are mentioned. /remind sets reminders (me, list, cancel). /temprole gives a member a role for a set time and takes it back when it expires.

Getting Started

  1. Invite LyrixBot and give it the permissions for the features you plan to use (for example Ban Members for /ban).
  2. Open the dashboard, choose your server and follow the Setup Wizard.
  3. Post the panels you need with /setup-tickets, /setup-ticket-departments post, /setup-verification, /setup-apply and /setup-islamic. Commands like /setup-tickets, /setup-verification and /server-backup are limited to administrators by default.
  4. Use Command Management in the dashboard to restrict commands to the channels and roles you want.

Questions, bug reports or suggestions are welcome in the support server.
